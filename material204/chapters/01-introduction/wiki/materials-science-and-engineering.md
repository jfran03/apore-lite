# Materials Science and Engineering

## Definition
Materials science investigates relationships between structures and properties of materials, and designs/develops new materials. Materials engineering creates products from existing materials and develops materials processing techniques.
> Source: 2.PNG

## Key Concepts
- **Composition:** Chemical make-up of a material.
  > Source: 2.PNG
- **Structure:** Arrangement of atoms in a material.
  > Source: 2.PNG
- **Processing:** Ways to shape materials into useful components.
  > Source: 2.PNG
- **Composition → Structure → Processing → Properties → Performance chain:** Composition leads to structure, which leads to processing, which leads to properties, which leads to performance.
  > Source: 2.PNG
- **Materials knowledge spectrum:** Materials science provides basic knowledge of materials; materials engineering provides applied knowledge of materials; together they produce resultant knowledge of the structure, properties, processing, and performance of engineering materials, which engineers use to convert materials into the products needed by society.
  > Source: 6.PNG

## Examples
- A disk made of aluminum oxide was shown in three forms — single crystal, polycrystalline, and polycrystalline with many pores — each having different optical properties despite identical composition, illustrating that structure (not just composition) determines properties.
