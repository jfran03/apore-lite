# Other Materials

## Definition
Beyond metals, ceramics, and polymers, materials also include composites and advanced materials.
> Source: 11.PNG

## Key Concepts
- **Composites:** A combination of two or more different materials.
  > Source: 11.PNG
- **Composite properties:** Composites combine the properties of the two (or more) materials they are formed from.
  > Source: 12.PNG
- **Advanced materials:** Includes semiconductors, biomaterials, smart materials, and nanoengineered materials.
  > Source: 11.PNG
- **Semiconductors:** Have conductivity in between ceramics and metals; used in computers, electronics, and micro-electrical-mechanical systems (MEMS).
  > Source: 13.PNG
- **Semiconductor examples:** Silicon, germanium, gallium arsenide.
  > Source: 13.PNG

## Examples
- Composite examples: aircraft equipment, bicycles, tennis rackets. - Semiconductors are used in solar panels, illustrated by a photovoltaic roof installation.
