# Why Materials Are Important

## Definition
Materials drive advancements in society, and it is important for engineers to understand materials so they can select appropriate materials and processing techniques for specific applications.
> Source: 4.PNG
> Source: 5.PNG

## Key Concepts
- **Historical materials ages:** Society's advancement has been marked by materials ages — the Stone Age, Bronze Age, and Iron Age.
  > Source: 4.PNG
- **Today's material age (open question):** The source poses today's material age as an open question, suggesting candidates such as the Silicon (Electronic Materials) Age, the Nano-materials Age, or the Polymer Age.
  > Source: 4.PNG
- **Products are made of materials:** Products, devices, and components that engineers design are all made of materials.
  > Source: 5.PNG
- **Engineer's requirement:** To select appropriate materials and processing techniques for specific applications, engineers must have knowledge of material properties and understand structure-property relationships.
  > Source: 5.PNG
