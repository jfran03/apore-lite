# Types of Materials

## Definition
Materials are broadly classified into metals and alloys, ceramics/glasses, and polymers/plastics.
> Source: 7.PNG

## Key Concepts
- **Metals and alloys:** Pure metallic elements or combinations of metallic elements (alloys); have a large number of de-localized electrons and therefore can conduct electricity.
  > Source: 7.PNG
- **Alloy:** A metal that contains additions of one or more metals or non-metals.
  > Source: 8.PNG
- **Steel:** An alloy of iron and a small content of carbon.
  > Source: 8.PNG
- **Brass:** An alloy of copper and zinc.
  > Source: 8.PNG
- **Metal examples:** Aluminum, magnesium, zinc, iron, titanium, beryllium-copper, nickel, copper-nickel, niobium.
  > Source: 8.PNG
- **Metal properties:** Good conductivity (both thermal and electricity), high strength, high stiffness, ductility.
  > Source: 8.PNG
- **Ceramics/glasses:** Compounds of metallic and non-metallic elements (oxides, carbides, nitrides, sulfides); generally insulating and refractory.
  > Source: 7.PNG
- **Ceramics:** Inorganic non-metallic materials, crystalline in structure (polycrystalline); examples include sand and rocks; properties include insulating (non-conductive), high hardness, strength and melting point, and being very brittle.
  > Source: 9.PNG
- **Glasses:** Amorphous materials with structure-dependent properties, which can be processed to yield an improvement in properties.
  > Source: 9.PNG
- **Polymers/plastics:** Chemical composition including carbon, hydrogen, and other non-metals; large molecular structure; flexible, lightweight, and easily processed.
  > Source: 7.PNG
- **Polymer terminology:** "Poly" means many, "mer" means unit; polymers are large molecular structures formed from monomers through polymerization.
  > Source: 10.PNG
- **Synthetic polymers:** Plastics such as polyethylene (PE), polymethyl methacrylate (PMMA), and polycarbonate.
  > Source: 10.PNG
- **Natural polymers:** Cellulose, glucan.
  > Source: 10.PNG
