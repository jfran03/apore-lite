## Q001
**Status:** active
**Type:** conceptual
**Difficulty:** introductory
**Topic:** materials-science-and-engineering
**Focus Area:** definitions of materials science vs materials engineering
**Question:** What is the difference between materials science and materials engineering?
**Answer:** Materials science investigates relationships between structure and properties of materials and designs/develops new materials, while materials engineering creates products from existing materials and develops materials processing techniques. (See materials-science-and-engineering.md)

## Q018
**Status:** active
**Type:** short-answer
**Difficulty:** advanced
**Topic:** material-property-comparisons
**Focus Area:** Ashby map purpose
**Question:** What is an Ashby map used for, and what two properties does the example map plot?
**Answer:** An Ashby map is used for two-property optimization — comparing and selecting materials based on two properties simultaneously. The example plots stiffness (elastic/Young's modulus, in GPa) against density (kg/m³), both on logarithmic scales, grouping materials into regions such as technical ceramics, composites, metals, natural materials, polymers, foams, and elastomers. (See material-property-comparisons.md)
