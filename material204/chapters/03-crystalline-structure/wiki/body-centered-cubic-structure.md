# Body-Centered Cubic (BCC) Structure

## Definition

The BCC unit cell has eight corner atoms and a single center atom.
> Source: F26-D2L-Chapter 3 - Crystalline Structure-Part 1.pdf (slide 13)

## Key Concepts

- **Atoms per unit cell:** N = N_i + N_f/2 + N_c/8 = 1 + 0 + 8/8 = **2** (1 center + 8 corners × 1/8).
  > Source: F26-D2L-Chapter 3 - Crystalline Structure-Part 1.pdf (slides 13, 14, 21)

- **Coordination number = 8.** Each center atom is surrounded by 8 other nearest-neighbour atoms.
  > Source: F26-D2L-Chapter 3 - Crystalline Structure-Part 1.pdf (slides 14, 21)

- **Where atoms touch:** atoms touch each other along the cube (body) diagonals.
  > Source: F26-D2L-Chapter 3 - Crystalline Structure-Part 1.pdf (slides 14, 16)

- **Lattice constant:** √3 a = 4r, so a = 4r/√3 and r = √3 a/4. Derived from (4R)² = a² + (√2a)² → 16R² = 3a² → a = 4R/√3.
  > Source: F26-D2L-Chapter 3 - Crystalline Structure-Part 1.pdf (slides 15, 21)

- **Volume of unit cell:** V = a³ = (4r/√3)³ = 64r³/(3√3).
  > Source: F26-D2L-Chapter 3 - Crystalline Structure-Part 1.pdf (slide 15)

- **Volume of atoms in unit cell:** 2 × (4/3)πr³ = (8/3)πr³.
  > Source: F26-D2L-Chapter 3 - Crystalline Structure-Part 1.pdf (slide 15)

- **Atomic packing factor:** APF = [(4/3)πR³ × 2] / (4R/√3)³ = √3π/6 ≈ **0.68**.
  > Source: F26-D2L-Chapter 3 - Crystalline Structure-Part 1.pdf (slides 15, 21)

## Examples

- Metals with the BCC structure named in the slides: Cr, W, Fe (α), Tantalum, Molybdenum.
  > Source: F26-D2L-Chapter 3 - Crystalline Structure-Part 1.pdf (slide 14)

- α-Fe (ferrite) is BCC below 912 °C, and δ-Fe is BCC between 1394 °C and 1538 °C.
  > Source: F26-D2L-Chapter 3 - Crystalline Structure-Part 1.pdf (slide 43)

## Common Misconceptions

- All atoms are identical; the center atom is shaded differently only for ease of viewing.
