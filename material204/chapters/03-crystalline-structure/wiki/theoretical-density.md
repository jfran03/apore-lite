# Theoretical Density

## Definition

> Density = ρ = (Mass of Atoms in Unit Cell) / (Total Volume of Unit Cell) = nA / (V_C N_A)

where
- n = number of atoms/unit cell
- A = atomic weight (g·mol⁻¹)
- V_C = Volume of unit cell = a³ for cubic
- N_A = Avogadro's number = 6.022 × 10²³ atoms/mol
> Source: F26-D2L-Chapter 3 - Crystalline Structure-Part 1.pdf (slides 30, 31)

## Key Concepts

- **Where the formula comes from:** one mole contains N_A atoms and has mass A, therefore the mass of one atom is A/N_A. Multiplying by the number of atoms in the cell gives the mass of one unit cell as nA/N_A. Dividing by the cell's volume gives ρ = nA/(N_A V_c).
  > Source: F26-D2L-Chapter 3 - Crystalline Structure-Part 1.pdf (slide 30)

- **Rearranged for n:** n = ρ V N_A / A.
  > Source: F26-D2L-Chapter 3 - Crystalline Structure-Part 1.pdf (slide 31)

- **Lattice constant substitutions:** a = 2r for SC, a = 4r/√3 for BCC, a = 2√2 r for FCC.
  > Source: F26-D2L-Chapter 3 - Crystalline Structure-Part 1.pdf (slide 31)

- **How to identify the structure from density data:**
  1. Compute n using the given data.
  2. Compare with expected values: n ≈ 1 ⇒ SC, n ≈ 2 ⇒ BCC, n ≈ 4 ⇒ FCC.
  > Source: F26-D2L-Chapter 3 - Crystalline Structure-Part 1.pdf (slide 31)

- **Specific volume per unit mass:** since ρ = m/V, rearranging gives V/m = 1/ρ. This is the route used to compare volumes of the same amount of material in two different crystal structures.
  > Source: F26-D2L-Chapter 3 - Crystalline Structure-Part 1.pdf (slide 48)

- **Percent volume change:** %ΔV = [(V_final − V_initial) / V_initial] × 100.
  > Source: F26-D2L-Chapter 3 - Crystalline Structure-Part 1.pdf (slide 49)

- **Unit conversion used in the worked problems:** 1 nm = 10⁻⁷ cm.
  > Source: F26-D2L-Chapter 3 - Crystalline Structure-Part 1.pdf (slide 45)
