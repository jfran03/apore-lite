# Simple Cubic (SC) Structure

## Definition

A simple cubic unit cell has atoms at the eight corners only. The number of atoms in the unit cell is the number of atoms in each corner × the contribution of each corner atom to the unit cell: 8 × 1/8 = 1.
> Source: F26-D2L-Chapter 3 - Crystalline Structure-Part 1.pdf (slide 9)

## Key Concepts

- **Atoms per unit cell:** N = N_i + N_f/2 + N_c/8 = 0 + 0 + 8/8 = **1**
  > Source: F26-D2L-Chapter 3 - Crystalline Structure-Part 1.pdf (slides 9, 21)

- **Coordination number = 6** (number of nearest neighbors).
  > Source: F26-D2L-Chapter 3 - Crystalline Structure-Part 1.pdf (slides 10, 21)

- **Where atoms touch:** close-packed directions are the cube edges; atoms touch along the edge.
  > Source: F26-D2L-Chapter 3 - Crystalline Structure-Part 1.pdf (slides 10, 16)

- **Lattice constant:** a = 2r (equivalently a = 2R).
  > Source: F26-D2L-Chapter 3 - Crystalline Structure-Part 1.pdf (slides 12, 16, 21)

- **Volume of unit cell:** V = a³ = (2r)³ = 8r³.
  > Source: F26-D2L-Chapter 3 - Crystalline Structure-Part 1.pdf (slide 12)

- **Volume of atoms in unit cell:** 1 × (4/3)πr³ = (4/3)πr³.
  > Source: F26-D2L-Chapter 3 - Crystalline Structure-Part 1.pdf (slide 12)

- **Atomic packing factor:** APF = [(4/3)πR³ × 1] / (8R³) = π/6 ≈ **0.52**.
  > Source: F26-D2L-Chapter 3 - Crystalline Structure-Part 1.pdf (slides 12, 21)

- **Rarity:** rare due to low packing density — only Po (Polonium-84) has this structure.
  > Source: F26-D2L-Chapter 3 - Crystalline Structure-Part 1.pdf (slide 10)

## Examples

- Polonium (Po, element 84) is the only material named in the slides as having the simple cubic structure.
  > Source: F26-D2L-Chapter 3 - Crystalline Structure-Part 1.pdf (slide 10)

## Common Misconceptions

- In the diagrams, spheres are reduced for visibility. Nearest neighbours touch in the ideal hard-sphere model.
