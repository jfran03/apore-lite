**Answer:** ρ = nA / (V_C N_A), where n = number of atoms per unit cell, A = atomic weight (g/mol), V_C = volume of the unit cell (a³ for cubic), and N_A = Avogadro's number = 6.022 × 10²³ atoms/mol. (theoretical-density.md)

## Q037
**Status:** active
**Type:** conceptual
**Difficulty:** advanced
**Topic:** theoretical-density
**Focus Area:** Deriving the density formula
**Question:** Explain how ρ = nA/(N_A V_c) is built up from the mass of a single atom.
**Answer:** One mole contains N_A atoms and has mass A, so the mass of one atom is A/N_A. Multiplying by the number of atoms in the cell gives the mass of one unit cell as nA/N_A. Dividing by the cell's volume gives ρ = nA/(N_A V_c). (theoretical-density.md)
