# AI and ML Overview

## Definition
**Artificial Intelligence (AI):** Any technique which enables computers to mimic human intelligence.
> Source: Copy of Section 1 _ Introduction to AI and Algoverse.pdf

**Machine Learning (ML):** Statistical techniques which enable computers to improve with experience. A subset of AI.
> Source: Copy of Section 1 _ Introduction to AI and Algoverse.pdf

**Deep Learning:** A subset of ML which makes computations using neural networks.
> Source: Copy of Section 1 _ Introduction to AI and Algoverse.pdf

## Key Concepts

- **Hierarchy:** Deep Learning ⊂ Machine Learning ⊂ Artificial Intelligence. The sources focus on Deep Learning / neural networks.
  > Source: Copy of Section 1 _ Introduction to AI and Algoverse.pdf

- **ML formal definition (Oxford Reference):** "A branch of artificial intelligence concerned with the construction of programs that learn from experience." Core takeaway: learning from data.
  > Source: Copy of Section 1 _ Introduction to AI and Algoverse.pdf

- **ML as applied statistics:** ML is often described as applied statistics and as a way to get computers to complete a specific task — like traditional programming, but with the intelligence built in via learning rather than explicit rules.
  > Source: Copy of Section 1 _ Introduction to AI and Algoverse.pdf

- **Why ML instead of traditional programming?** Traditional programming requires telling the computer exact steps. ML instead shows the computer examples (data) and uses an algorithm to learn from that data. This learning process is called **training**.
  > Source: Copy of Section 1 _ Introduction to AI and Algoverse.pdf

- **Data and labels:** For most ML models, data must be labeled — each data point needs a ground truth label (e.g., not just a picture of a dog, but also the label "dog"). Within reason, data is more important than small changes to the method.
  > Source: Copy of Section 1 _ Introduction to AI and Algoverse.pdf

## Examples
- ChatGPT predicts the next word in a sentence.
- Image generators like DALL-E/Stable Diffusion/Midjourney predict from a text prompt what the corresponding image should look like.
