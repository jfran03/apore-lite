# calculus275

## Subject Scope
An extensive treatment of differential and integral calculus in a single variable, with an emphasis on applications. Differentiation: derivative laws, the mean value theorem, optimization, curve sketching and other applications. Integral calculus: the fundamental theorem of calculus, techniques of integration, improper integrals, and areas of planar regions. Infinite series: power series, Taylor's theorem and Taylor series.

## Goal
general mastery — deep conceptual command of single-variable calculus, not tied to a particular exam date. Understanding *why* the theorems hold and when they apply matters as much as computing correctly.

## Tutor Style
rigorous — precise and proof-aware. State hypotheses explicitly and check that they hold before applying a theorem (continuity vs. differentiability, closed vs. open intervals, convergence conditions). Use formal language and correct notation. Do not let a right answer obtained by a wrong or unjustified argument pass as correct.
