# Derivatives of Exponential and Logarithmic Functions

## Definition
Let f(x) = aˣ where a > 0 and a ≠ 1. Applying the first-principle definition:

F'(x) = lim_{h→0} [a^(x+h) - aˣ] / h = aˣ · lim_{h→0} [a^h - 1] / h
> Source: Week 1 Video - Tangent Lines, the Derivative and its Basic Laws.pdf

The limit lim_{h→0} [a^h - 1] / h equals ln(a), so (aˣ)' = ln(a) · aˣ.
> Source: Week 1 Video - Tangent Lines, the Derivative and its Basic Laws.pdf

## Key Concepts
- **Derivative of eˣ:** since ln(e) = 1, the special case a = e gives (eˣ)' = eˣ.
  > Source: Week 1 Video - Tangent Lines, the Derivative and its Basic Laws.pdf
- **Derivative of logarithms:** (log_a x)' = 1/(x ln a). In particular, (ln x)' = 1/x.
  > Source: Week 1 Video - Tangent Lines, the Derivative and its Basic Laws.pdf
