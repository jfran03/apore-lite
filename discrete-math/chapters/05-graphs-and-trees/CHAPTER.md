# Chapter 5: Graphs and Trees

**Domain:** Discrete Math
**Topics:** A graph is a set of points (called nodes) connected by lines (called edges). A tree is a special kind of graph with no cycles, like a family tree or a folder structure on your computer. These structures help us model networks, relationships and hierarchies. In this module, you'll explore how to represent graphs and trees, identify their properties and use them to model real-world computing problems.
