# Chapter 3: Number Theory Principles

**Domain:** Discrete Math
**Topics:** Number theory is the study of integers (whole numbers like −5, 0 and 42) and their properties. For example, prime numbers (like 2, 3, 5, 7...) are important in both mathematics and real-world applications like cryptography. Understanding how numbers behave gives us the foundation for secure systems and smart algorithms. In this module, you'll explore concepts like divisibility, prime factorization, greatest common divisors and modular arithmetic, which are all tools used in computer science and encryption.
