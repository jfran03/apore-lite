# Prime, Composite, and Relatively Prime Numbers

## Definition

**Prime Number.** A prime number is an integer greater than 1 that is divisible only by 1 and itself. For a prime p, the only positive integers that divide p evenly are 1 and p. Examples: 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19.
> Source: Prime, Composite, and Relatively Prime Numbers.html

**Composite Number.** A composite number is an integer greater than 1 that is not prime; it can be written as a product of smaller positive integers. Examples: 4, 6, 8, 9, 10, 12, 15, 20.
> Source: Prime, Composite, and Relatively Prime Numbers.html

**Relatively Prime (Coprime) Numbers.** Two positive integers a and b are relatively prime if their greatest common divisor is 1: `gcd(a, b) = 1`. Examples: 8 and 15 (gcd = 1); 9 and 10 (gcd = 1).
> Source: Prime, Composite, and Relatively Prime Numbers.html

## Key Concepts

- **Primes as building blocks:** Prime numbers are the most basic building blocks of all integers — just as molecules are made of atoms, composite numbers are made of primes.
  > Source: Prime, Composite, and Relatively Prime Numbers.html
- **Why 1 is neither:** 1 is not prime because it has only one divisor (itself); a prime must be greater than 1. 1 is neither prime nor composite.
  > Source: Prime, Composite, and Relatively Prime Numbers.html
- **Composite structure:** Each composite number can be broken down into a unique combination of prime factors.
  > Source: Prime, Composite, and Relatively Prime Numbers.html
- **Relatively prime pairs need not be prime themselves:** e.g., 8 and 15 are both composite but are relatively prime because they share no common factor except 1. Relatively prime numbers play a key role in modular arithmetic.
  > Source: Prime, Composite, and Relatively Prime Numbers.html

## Examples

- **Prime:** 19 is prime because it is divisible only by 1 and itself.
  > Source: Prime, Composite, and Relatively Prime Numbers.html
- **Not prime:** 4 = 2 × 2 and 9 = 3 × 3, so both are composite.
  > Source: Prime, Composite, and Relatively Prime Numbers.html
- **Not relatively prime:** 6 and 9 → gcd = 3; 12 and 18 → gcd = 6.
  > Source: Prime, Composite, and Relatively Prime Numbers.html

## Did You Know

- 2 is the only even prime number; every other even number is divisible by 2 and therefore composite.
- There are infinitely many prime numbers. Euclid proved this by showing that multiplying all known primes and adding 1 always generates a new prime or a number with a new prime factor.
- The largest known prime number has more than 24 million digits.
  > Source: Prime, Composite, and Relatively Prime Numbers.html
