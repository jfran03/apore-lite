## Source Notes

The five `.html` lessons are the course's online learning pages; the four `.pdf` files are the instructor's lecture notes (largely handwritten). The PDFs cover material the HTML pages do not — Bayes' theorem and contingency tables, inclusion–exclusion for three and more sets, the binomial and Poisson distributions, the normal distribution and z-scores, and the Central Limit Theorem. Several PDF problems are stated in the source without worked solutions; these are recorded as posed and are not used as model answers.

The 11 `Capture*.PNG` files are worksheet screenshots. They contain **problems only — no answers, no new definitions or formulas**. They introduced no new topic pages; each problem was folded into the wiki page whose method it exercises, under a "Worksheet Problems" heading. Because the screenshots state no solutions, every worked answer is derived by applying a rule already documented in this wiki from the `.html`/`.pdf` sources, and each is cited in two parts: `Capture{N}.PNG (problem as posed)` for the setup, and the originating source for the method used. Where the wiki does not settle a problem — parts (a), (b) and (c) of the dependent/independent exercise in Capture7.PNG — it is recorded as posed and left unanswered rather than resolved from outside knowledge. Capture4.PNG duplicates a birthday problem already compiled from Probability - Copy.pdf and was added as a co-citation on the existing example rather than as new content.
